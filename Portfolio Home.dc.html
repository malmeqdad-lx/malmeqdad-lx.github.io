The notation I design in

Every spec I write uses one shape glossary, published on page one of the document. It exists so that a developer, a QA analyst, and a business stakeholder can read the same diagram and argue about the same thing.

The distinction that matters most is the thin border versus the thick one. A thin box plays information and moves on. A thick box waits for a human, which means it needs an error prompt, a retry, and somewhere to go when the retry fails. If a designer forgets to define those, the shape itself gives them away.

Drawn in Lucidchart and Visio; specifications delivered alongside sample dialogues and wireframes.
