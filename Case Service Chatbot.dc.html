Pet insurance carrier · Web and mobile

Warmth that survives contact with a claims API

The brand voice on the website is playful — furry friends, fetch, best buddy. That voice is charming when you are buying a policy and unbearable when you are filing a claim for an animal that is dying. The design problem was one persona that could hold both.

Role

Persona definition, dialogue specification, tone rules, error handling.

Goals

Reduce agent transfers, increase automation, across quoting, coverage, claims and payments.

Two audiences

Unauthenticated shoppers on the public site; policyholders in the authenticated app.

Design from volume

Before writing a line of dialogue I pulled the agent disposition counts for a quarter. Sales inquiries are what the business talks about; claims are what the queue is actually made of. Four of the top five reasons people contacted support were claim-related, which decided the build order and the emotional centre of gravity of the persona.

The tonal rule

Personality lives in explanation, never in prompting. When the assistant is describing something, it can sound like the brand. When it is asking a person for information, or delivering news, it gets out of the way. That single line resolved almost every tone argument in review, because it converts taste into a rule anyone can apply.

Moment Written Rejected
Quote intro Tell us a bit about your best friend and we'll put a quote together. Please provide your pet's details to begin the quotation process.
Claim in review Your claim is with a reviewer. They usually finish within three business days, and you'll get an email the moment it's decided. Let me fetch that for you! Your claim is still being sniffed out by our team.
Claim denied This one wasn't covered, and I'm sorry — the reason given was a pre-existing condition. You can appeal it, and I can start that now. Unfortunately your claim has been denied. Is there anything else I can help with?
Asking for a policy number What's the policy number? It's at the top of any email we've sent you. Pawlicy number, please!

Also decided

Two entry experiences, one persona

The public site is unauthenticated and mostly shopping; the app is authenticated and mostly service. The assistant greets by name in one and not the other, and the authenticated side carries noticeably more empathy, because the people there already have something at stake.

Echo before acting

On anything urgent the assistant repeats back what it understood before doing it. It costs a line and it is the cheapest insurance against a confidently wrong action on someone's claim.

API failure is a designed state

Every data call in the specification has a written failure prompt. Systems go down during exactly the conversations where the user is least able to absorb a generic apology.
