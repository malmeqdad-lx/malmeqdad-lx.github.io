Finance and procurement, without the portal

An assistant inside the chat client employees already live in, handling purchase requests, invoice questions and policy guidance. The interesting constraint was not what it could do. It was deciding what it must refuse to do, in a domain where a confident wrong answer becomes a compliance finding.

Role

Conversation design lead on the dialog specification: intents, scope, guardrails, escalation.

Surface

A workplace chat client, where the assistant sits beside colleagues and inherits their conversational expectations.

Stakes

Spend approvals and policy interpretation. Being wrong is more expensive than being unhelpful.

Earning the right to answer

The assistant has three tiers of answer, and it only descends a tier when the one above it has genuinely failed. Known intents resolve deterministically against systems of record. Unmatched questions fall to retrieval-grounded generation over approved policy documents. When retrieval finds nothing relevant, the assistant says so rather than composing something plausible out of the general knowledge in the model.

01

High-confidence intent match

Answered from the system of record, with the deterministic flow the specification defines. No generation involved.

02

Low confidence, retrieval succeeds

Generated from retrieved policy content only, carrying a visible marker that the answer was AI-generated and a link to the source document.

03

Retrieval comes back empty

A distinct path, not a generic fallback. The assistant names what it looked in, and routes to the team that owns the question.

The disclosure marker was non-negotiable for me. If an employee cannot tell whether they are reading policy or paraphrase, they will quote the paraphrase in an audit.

Tier 02 as rendered · disclosure pattern
Do I need three quotes for a $40k software renewal?

Competitive bids are required above $25,000, so yes — three, unless the vendor is sole-source approved. Renewals with an existing master agreement can use the one-quote exception.

The marker and the citation are one unit and cannot be rendered separately. A disclosure without a source tells the employee to distrust the answer with no way to resolve it.

Scope, written down

Answers

Status of a purchase order or invoice

Who approves a request at a given amount

What a policy says, quoted with its source

How to start a process, step by step

Refuses, and routes

Whether an exception to policy is allowed

Anything that reads as approving spend

Interpretation of a contract or legal term

Anything about a colleague's compensation

Failing twice

Repeating a fallback verbatim is the fastest way to make software feel unoccupied. The second miss admits the problem is not going to be solved by trying again.

Miss 1

I didn't quite get that. Are you asking about an invoice, a purchase request, or a policy?

Miss 2

I'm not going to get this one. I can pass it to the procurement team with what you've written so far — want me to?

The handoff carries the transcript. Making the employee retype their question to a human is the moment they stop using the assistant.

Position

Scope is a conversation design deliverable, not a product decision handed down to me. The list of things an assistant refuses is written in the same document as the things it says, because both are language.
